# Clause positions — default stance for a small contractor

Use as a starting point, then adjust to the firm's facts. "Ask" is a question to the
agency; "Fallback" is what to accept if the ask fails. Replacement language is a
suggestion for counsel, never a finished clause. Legal effect of any statute named here is
`unverified` until a primary source is read; where the network blocks sources, say so.

| Clause type | Red-flag wording | Why it bites | Default position | Ask / assumption | Replacement idea (for counsel) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Indemnity, broad form | covers loss "regardless of whether caused in part by the negligence of" the agency | firm insures the agency's fault; uninsurable in part; can exceed policy limits | Counsel + Negotiate | "Does the agency intend the indemnity to cover its own negligence?" Assume limited to the contractor's acts. | Indemnify for claims "to the extent caused by" the contractor's (and its subcontractors') negligence or breach. Texas has a statute on construction indemnity (Insurance Code ch. 151, `unverified`): ask counsel whether it reaches this contract and whether "to the extent permitted by law" saves the clause. |
| Second indemnity hidden under another heading | "Limitation of Agreement" heading, but any third-party claim is indemnified, no fault test | unbounded, not tied to fault | Negotiate | Ask whether it is meant to add to the first. | Delete, or merge into the first with the same fault test. |
| "Save harmless" in general conditions | open-ended "claims of every description" | stacks on the sample-contract indemnity | Clarify | Ask which indemnity controls when they differ (order of precedence). | One indemnity, one standard. |
| Payment "on account only, subject to adjustment after audit" | no look-back limit | agency can reclaim years later | Negotiate | Ask the audit look-back period. Assume the records-retention period (3 years). | Adjustments limited to the retention period; disputed items itemized in writing. |
| Payment terms | net 45 after invoice "in proper form" + review | cash-flow strain for a young firm | Price it + Clarify | Ask whether a prompt-payment statute applies (`unverified`) and what "proper form" means. | Net 30; interest on late payment if the statute allows; deemed-approved after a stated review window. |
| Not-to-exceed amount blank, no volume guarantee | "as needed" work | no revenue floor; capacity planned on hope | Clarify + Price it | Ask estimated annual volume and any cap. Assume no minimum; price mobilization into unit prices. | Minimum work or standby fee only if the agency will agree (rarely). |
| Termination for convenience | payment "only for services rendered", short or no notice | stranded crew, materials, demobilization | Negotiate | Ask notice period. Assume 30 days. | Pay for work performed and accepted, committed materials and reasonable demobilization. |
| Termination for default | agency finishes work, contractor pays the extra cost, no cure period, offset | unbounded | Negotiate | Ask cure period. | Written notice and a cure period (e.g. 10 days); extra cost capped at the price difference for the remaining work. |
| Changes clause | contractor must assert an adjustment within 30 days of the written order | easy to lose money by missing it | Accept + Process | Add a calendar control: log every written order and diarize day 25. | Longer window (e.g. 60 days) if the agency will move. |
| Time is of the essence + hard service levels | 24-hour start, 72-hour finish, no relief | breach risk from agency access delays, key availability, occupied units | Clarify + Price it | Define "hours" (clock vs business). Relief for agency-caused delay and no-access. | Clock stops for agency-caused delay; completion measured from access. |
| Correction "at no additional charge" | unlimited rework | scope creep disguised as defects | Clarify | Ask standard of acceptance and baseline. | Acceptance against a written checklist and baseline photos; correction within a stated period. |
| Deemed acceptance | work accepted if agency silent 30 days — but only for some deliverables | one-way | Accept | Note it; do not rely on it. | Mutual deemed acceptance for completed units after the walk-through. |
| Damage to "work of other trades" / "like new" repair | contractor repairs any damage "in any manner" to like-new | no baseline: pre-existing damage becomes contractor's | Price it + Process | Ask for a pre-work condition report or allow the contractor's. | Baseline photos at start; pre-existing conditions excluded. |
| Open-ended scope | "repairs identified by the agency" | unpriced work | Clarify | Ask that unpriced items go through a written change or a unit price. | Additional work only by written order at a listed or agreed price (the changes clause already says no extra charge without written consent). |
| Warranty undefined | "limited warranty" with no term | unknown exposure | Clarify | Ask the required period and remedy. | State term and exclusions (misuse, wear). |
| Materials by the agency vs price schedule asking materials | "agency furnishes paint" but the schedule says "labor + materials" | price basis unclear; double-counting or loss | Clarify (priority) | Ask which lines include materials. Assume the agency furnishes what the scope says. | Schedule annotated line by line. |
| Insurance | obsolete form edition; 30-day cancellation endorsement; professional liability on a trades contract; A-: VII carriers; waiver of subrogation | may be unobtainable or unnecessary; cost | Clarify + Price it | See `insurance-and-flowdowns.md`. | Accept "or broader"; 30 days' notice "to the extent available"; delete inapplicable coverage. |
| Order of precedence | contract controls over the proposal | proposal assumptions have no force | Process | Put assumptions through the question window so they enter the record as addenda. | Addenda and accepted Q&A part of the contract documents. |
| Records and audit | agency, federal agencies and comptroller may examine records; retention period; must flow to subs over a threshold | compliance burden | Accept + Process | Keep records per job; flow-down in subcontracts. | — |
| Section 3 / M/WBE | reporting "requirements" not attached; grounds for termination | uncertain duties | Clarify | Ask the reporting format and regime (older or current rule). | Attach the format before signing. |
| Labor standards | maintenance-wage clauses referenced but not included | changes every labor price | Clarify (top priority) | Ask whether prevailing maintenance wage rates apply and request the decision. | — |
| Assignment and teaming | no assignment; partnership survivorship language | constrains teaming | Clarify | Ask whether subcontracting and teaming are allowed and how they are approved. | Consent not unreasonably withheld. |
| Liens | contractor and subs may not lien agency property | sub payment risk; no lien remedy | Accept + Process | Ask whether a payment bond is required. | Pay-when-paid only with counsel. |
| Venue / governing law | blank or stale in the sample | uncertainty | Read the PDF first | Confirm it reads what you think. | — |
| Disputes | 30 days to except to a decision; suit within one year; keep working meanwhile | short clocks | Accept + Process | Diarize dates. | Longer clocks if offered. |

## When to recommend not signing
The indemnity is unlimited and uninsurable, AND the agency will not discuss it; the insurance
cannot be obtained before award by any broker; the firm cannot staff the service level the
contract makes a condition of payment; or a required certification would be false.
