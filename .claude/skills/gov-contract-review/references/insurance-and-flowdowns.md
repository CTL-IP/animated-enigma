# Insurance gap table and flow-down checklist

## Insurance: one row per requirement
Columns: requirement (quote + location) · firm's actual status · action · who · broker wording.
Status values: **verified** (a certificate or declarations page was read) · **claimed** (only a
marketing line or memory) · **no evidence** · **not applicable (ask)**. Never write "claimed" as
"verified".

Typical requirements and what to check:
- **Commercial general liability** — limits per occurrence/aggregate; occurrence form; the named
  ISO form edition (old editions are common in stale templates — "or broader" is usually accepted);
  products/completed operations; additional insured with no special limitations; primary and
  non-contributory; waiver of subrogation; deductibles limited to property damage.
- **Workers' compensation and employers' liability** — statutory plus stated employers' limits; state
  listing on the declarations page; waiver of subrogation. Confirm coverage exists for every employee
  and how subcontractors are covered. (State rules are `unverified` until read.)
- **Automobile** — combined single limit; any-auto symbol; hired and non-owned; additional insured.
  Check who owns the vehicles and whether the policy names them.
- **Professional liability** — often copied from a consulting contract; ask whether it applies to a
  trades contract; if required, claims-made tail periods.
- **Umbrella/excess** — only if required; often used to reach limits cheaply.
- **Carrier rating and licensing** — A.M. Best rating floor; carrier authorized in the state.
- **Notice of cancellation endorsement** — some agencies demand 30 days' written notice by endorsement;
  standard certificates say the insurer "will endeavor" to notify. Ask the broker whether the endorsement
  is available and its cost; ask the agency whether "endeavor" suffices.
- **Subcontractors** — added as additional insureds, or separate certificates; sub limits equal to the prime's.
- **Timing** — certificates and endorsements approved BEFORE services; ask the broker how long each takes.

Do not invent premiums or lead times; write "ask broker".

## Flow-down checklist for every subcontract
Compile from the solicitation's general conditions; for each clause write: *clause, threshold
(dollars), what the sub must do, where it sits in the subcontract*. Usual suspects:
records access and retention (first-tier subs above a stated amount) · equal employment and
disability clauses (subs above a stated amount) · Section 3 (with a preliminary statement of ability
to comply) · conflict-of-interest disclosure · lobbying certification · no liens · recovered materials ·
debarment/exclusion certification · insurance and additional-insured terms · wage and payroll terms if
labor standards apply · licence requirements for regulated trades. Keep the checklist with the
subcontract template so nothing is signed without it.

## Exclusion checks
Before anyone signs a debarment statement, run the exclusion searches the solicitation implies on the
firm, every principal, the project manager and every proposed subcontractor. The yes/no is the
signer's, never the assistant's.
