# Forms and certifications commonly found in housing-authority solicitations

Columns: what it is · who signs · what signing commits the firm to · common traps. Edition checks:
compare the printed edition and any OMB expiry to the issuing agency's current form and ask
whether the agency accepts older editions. Page numbers and exact wording come from the solicitation —
reproduce forms verbatim.

| Form | What it is | Who signs | What signing means | Traps |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **HUD-5369-B** Instructions to Offerors, Non-Construction | Bid-preparation and award rules: examine documents; submission; amendments; responsibility of the contractor; late offers; award to the responsible offeror whose offer is most advantageous; protests | nobody (it is instructions) | Submitting accepts them | Written for paper sealed bids and mailed late offers; the solicitation cover may say electronic only — the cover controls; ask. Award "on initial offers without discussions" is allowed, so the first offer must be the best. |
| **HUD-5370-C** General Conditions, Non-Construction (Section I, and Section II for maintenance contracts) | Standard contract clauses: changes, termination, records, rights in data, disputes, assignment, certificate and release, conflicts of interest, lobbying, EEO, disability, Section 3, recovered materials. Section II holds labor standards for maintenance contracts | it becomes part of the contract | Every clause, plus flow-down to subcontracts | The applicability note says which Sections apply by contract type and dollar value. If a required Section is not in the package (or a clause cites a missing "Section III"), ask before pricing labor. Clause deadlines (e.g. 30 days to assert an adjustment) are traps. |
| **Form HUD-50071** Certification of Payments to Influence Federal Transactions | Certifies that no federal appropriated funds were paid to influence a federal official on this award; other funds require a disclosure form | authorized official | A false certification carries civil penalties printed on the form | Check the edition and OMB expiry; confirm nobody was paid to lobby — paying an employee or a professional to prepare the bid is generally distinct (verify). |
| **Section 3 Business Concern / Program Certification** | Says the firm is, or is not, a Section 3 business, and which employment/contracting efforts it will make | authorized official | Checking an effort box is a **commitment**; claiming business status is an **attestation** that needs documents | The form may use a superseded definition; the contract may require reporting "within X days of award" to an unnamed recipient. Do not claim status without the documents the current rule requires. |
| **MBE / WBE / HUB status** | Self-declaration of minority-, woman-owned or historically underutilized status; may require certificates | authorized official | Attestation | Definitions turn on 51% ownership **and** control; unsettled or unsigned ownership makes the answer unanswerable. Third-party certificates take time. |
| **EEO / Employer Information Report** | Workforce table by job category, race/ethnicity and sex | authorized official | Factual workforce counts | A very small workforce makes individuals identifiable; count employees on the date stated, not "eventual" hires. |
| **Non-collusive affidavit** | Sworn statement: no one was paid to obtain the contract except regular employees; bid is genuine; no collusion; all statements true | principal, before a notary | Criminal exposure if false | Consultants and teaming partners may conflict with "no other person has an interest"; the form may not contemplate an LLC signer — ask who may sign. |
| **Profile of Firm** (with debarment statement) | Identity, ownership table, key personnel, debarment yes/no | authorized official | Accuracy of identity and ownership; the debarment answer | The debarment question can be unbounded ("ever", any principal, any government) — run the exclusion searches first. Legal name must match the state record. |
| **Business references** | Minimum number of references with contacts | — | Permission must be obtained | Do not list a reference you have not asked; related-party clients are not independent. |
| **Conflict of Interest Questionnaire (Texas Form CIQ)** | State form for vendors doing business with local government entities | the vendor | Disclosure of relationships with local government officers | The authority may have attached an old edition; check the state agency's current form and ask which it accepts. Filing deadlines can be tied to when the vendor becomes aware. |
| **Texas boycott / firearm-entity certification (Gov't Code ch. 2271 and 2274 as headed)** | Written verification that the contractor does not boycott Israel, does not boycott energy companies, and does not discriminate against firearm entities | authorized official | The form's own text states the thresholds (contract value and employee count) and offers a "does not apply" option | Which option is true depends on facts only the signer knows (employee count, contract value). The statute numbering may have changed since the form was printed (`search-excerpt`); ask. |
| **Standard Form LLL / lobbying disclosure** | Disclosure of non-federal lobbying payments | authorized official | Required only if such payments exist | Usually "none"; confirm. |
| **Insurance certificate (sample)** | ACORD sample showing what the authority expects | broker | — | Sample often shows requirements insurers will not meet (cancellation-notice promises). |

## Reading the contract for federal strings
Federal funding brings: records access for the authority and federal agencies for a stated period after
final payment; equal opportunity and disability clauses above thresholds; Section 3; no-lien and
conflict-of-interest clauses; debarment consequences; recovered-materials procurement; energy efficiency.
Collect the thresholds; they drive the flow-down checklist.
