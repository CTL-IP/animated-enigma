## How to close the gaps
With an unblocked connection: read the primary text for each row, change the status to
`confirmed-primary` with the date and URL, and delete or correct anything that does not hold. Ask the
authority's procurement office which regime and which form editions it applies — their answer, in an
addendum, is what binds the bid.
